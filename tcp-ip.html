Definition

Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) is a set of communication protocols used to connect devices and enable data exchange over the internet and other networks.

Explanation

TCP/IP works by dividing data into smaller packets that are sent across networks. TCP ensures that the data is delivered correctly and in the right order, while IP is responsible for addressing and routing the packets to their correct destination.

Importance

TCP/IP is important because it forms the foundation of internet communication. It allows different devices and systems to communicate reliably, making it possible to browse websites, send emails, and use online services.

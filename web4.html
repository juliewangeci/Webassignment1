Definition

Web 4.0 refers to the fourth stage of the World Wide Web, where advanced technologies such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, and the Internet of Things (IoT) are deeply integrated into online systems.

Explanation

Web 4.0 is designed to create an intelligent, connected web where devices, systems, and applications can communicate seamlessly. It aims to provide highly personalized and predictive user experiences by analysing user behaviour and connecting physical and digital environments.

Importance

Web 4.0 is important because it represents the future of the internet, enabling smarter automation, improved decision-making, and stronger integration between humans and technology across many industries.

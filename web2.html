Definition

Web 2.0 refers to the second stage of the World Wide Web, where websites became interactive and allowed users to create and share content.

Explanation

Unlike Web 1.0, Web 2.0 introduced dynamic and interactive features such as social media platforms, blogs, wikis, and video sharing sites. Users can now contribute content, comment, and collaborate online.

Importance

Web 2.0 is important because it transformed the internet into a participatory platform. It enabled communication, collaboration, and the rise of online communities and social networks.

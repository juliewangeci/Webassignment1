Definition

An extranet is a private network that uses Internet protocol communications to share information and resources between an organization and its partners or customers.

Explanation

Extranets allow organizations to securely share part of their information or operations with external parties, such as suppliers, vendors, or customers, while keeping the rest of their network private.

Importance

Extranets are important for improving collaboration and communication between businesses and their external stakeholders, enabling faster decision-making and more efficient business processes.

Definition

An extranet is a private network that uses internet technologies to share information and resources between an organization and external users such as partners, suppliers, or customers.

Explanation

An extranet extends a company internal network by allowing authorized external users to access certain parts of it. This access is controlled and secure, ensuring that only specific information is shared while the rest of the network remains protected.

Importance

Extranets are important because they improve communication and collaboration between businesses and external stakeholders. They help streamline operations, reduce delays, and make it easier to share important information securely.

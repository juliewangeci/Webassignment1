Definition

Multitier architecture is a client-server architecture in which the application is divided into multiple layers or tiers, each with a specific role and responsibility.

Explanation

In a multitier architecture, the application is organized into distinct layers, such as the presentation layer (client-side), the business logic layer (server-side), and the data layer (database). This separation of concerns allows for better organization, maintainability, and scalability of the application.

Importance

Multitier architecture is important in networking and web technology because it promotes a modular design that enhances flexibility, maintainability, and performance. It allows different teams to work on separate layers independently, facilitating easier development and debugging.
